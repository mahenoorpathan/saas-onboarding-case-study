# Support Ticket Triage Guide

How incoming client requests are handled after go-live, from the morning queue to a closed ticket.

## Where requests come from
- **Ticket system:** formal requests logged by the client
- **Email:** direct messages from the client

## Daily routine
1. **Morning queue:** the team lead reviews all new tickets and emails.
2. **Assignment:** each request is assigned to a team member.
3. **Flagging:** assigned emails are flagged so ownership is clear and nothing is picked up twice.

## Working a request

### Step 1: Read and classify
Open the ticket or email and decide what kind of request it is:

| Type | Example | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Query** | "How do I update a booking?" | Answer it, or point to the right section of the user guide |
| **Change** | "Please add a room" · "Change our room price" · "Update the menu prices" | Make the change in the software |

### Step 2: Act
- **Query:** reply with clear steps. If the same question keeps coming up, it's a sign the user guide needs updating.
- **Change:** make the update, then **check it works**. For example, make a test booking after a price change.

### Step 3: Close the loop
- Reply to the client confirming what was answered or changed.
- Close the ticket.

## Good habits
- Confirm the exact details before changing anything (which room, which price, from when).
- Check every change before telling the client it's done.
- Keep replies short and specific.
- Note repeated questions so documentation can be improved.
